Progress & Equipment

⭐ Clash Ninja

Web · Free · Bread Crew Pick

Probably the best place to start if you want to organize your village.

Track builders, laboratory upgrades and overall progression, check how long you have left to max, and explore useful guides for different parts of the game.

Best for: Everyday village progression

Open Clash Ninja →

⭐ Clash Max

Web · Free · Bread Crew Pick

Upgrade planning gets much more interesting here.

Instead of simply telling you how long everything takes, Clash Max can turn your village data into a realistic upgrade schedule based on builders, laboratory time, storage limits, priorities and even when you are actually available to play.

Best for: Players who want a real upgrade plan instead of just a timer

Open Clash Max →

⭐ Clash Calculator

Web · Free · Bread Crew Pick

A Clash of Clans toolbox packed into one website.

Plan upgrades, laboratory research, pets and Hero Equipment, estimate your future Ore income, compare upgrade paths and use combat calculators without jumping between multiple websites.

Best for: Players who love planning and numbers

Open Clash Calculator →

OreCalc

Web · Free

Hero Equipment can consume a lot of Ore very quickly. OreCalc helps you decide where that Ore should go.

Plan equipment upgrades, estimate Shiny, Glowy and Starry Ore income, organize upgrade priorities and see how long your equipment goals could take.

Best for: Hero Equipment and Ore planning

Open OreCalc →

Attacks & Armies

⭐ ClashArmies

Web · Free · Bread Crew Pick

Found a new strategy you want to try? Build the complete army before you even open the game.

Create compositions with troops, spells, siege machines, Clan Castle troops, heroes, pets and equipment, then save or share them with other players.

It is also a useful place to discover army ideas created by the community.

Best for: Building, testing and sharing army compositions

Open ClashArmies →

Clashify

Web · Free

A compact collection of useful Clash tools in one place.

One of the highlights is its ZapQuake calculator, which helps you work out combinations of Lightning, Earthquake and other damage sources against defenses.

It also includes village, equipment and war-related tools.

Best for: Quick attack calculations and smaller utilities

Open Clashify →

Bases & Scouting

⭐ Base Drop

Web · Freemium · Bread Crew Pick

This one is especially fun.

Take a screenshot of a base and upload it to Base Drop. The tool can search for matching layouts and help you find base links, attack references and strategies connected to similar bases.

Very useful when you see an interesting base in War, Ranked or a video and want to investigate it.

Best for: Scouting bases from screenshots

Open Base Drop →

ClashFox Attack Finder

Web · Free

Upload a screenshot of a base and ClashFox will search for real attacks against similar layouts.

Instead of only helping you identify the base, it can surface army compositions and source videos so you can study how comparable bases were attacked before planning your own hit.

You can also explore strategies by troop, check popular Legend League armies and search for base links.

Best for: Finding attack ideas from a base screenshot

Find Attacks →

Clash Champs — I Need a Base

Web · Free Library

Sometimes you do not want to build a base from scratch. You just want a good one.

Clash Champs has a large community library where you can browse bases by Town Hall and purpose, including War, CWL, Trophy, Farming and other styles.

Find something you like and use the available base link to bring it into Clash of Clans.

Best for: Quickly finding a new base layout

Find a Base →

War, CWL & Clan Stats

⭐ ClansHQ

Web · Free · No Account Required · Bread Crew Pick

One of the easiest ways to understand what is actually happening inside a clan.

Enter a clan tag and explore War, CWL and Capital Raid analytics, including participation, missed attacks, offensive and defensive performance and historical results.

No account. No setup. No subscription.

Best for: Quickly reviewing clan performance

Open ClansHQ →

Clanalyze

Web · Free

This is where clan data becomes a leadership tool.

Clanalyze focuses heavily on War and CWL performance, with historical analytics, missed attacks, player comparisons, performance metrics and a CWL roster planner.

If you help organize lineups or evaluate performance across a season, this is one of the most interesting tools on the list.

Best for: CWL planning and clan leadership

Open Clanalyze →

Clash of Stats

Web · Free

Sometimes the most useful information is not what a player is doing today, but what they have done before.

Clash of Stats is particularly useful for exploring player and clan history, rankings, previous clan memberships and long-term account information.

That makes it especially useful when recruiting or researching players.

Best for: Player history and recruitment research

Open Clash of Stats →

ClashSpot

Web · Free

Want to go deeper into Clash data?

ClashSpot brings together statistics and rankings for players, clans, Clan Wars, CWL, Clan Capital and Legend League.

It is particularly useful for exploring the wider game rather than only tracking your own account.

Best for: Legend League, rankings and global statistics

Open ClashSpot →

Discord & Clan Management

⭐ ClashKing

Discord · Web · Free · Bread Crew Pick

If your clan lives on Discord, this is worth exploring.

ClashKing goes far beyond simple player lookups. It can help manage clans, accounts, wars, CWL rosters, reminders, statistics and server automation through its Discord bot and web dashboard.

For leadership teams managing an active clan community, it can replace a lot of manual work.

Best for: Discord-based clan management

Open ClashKing →

Why These Tools?

We are not trying to list every Clash of Clans website ever created.

Bread Crew Tools is a curated list. We prioritize tools that are:

  • Genuinely useful during normal gameplay.
  • Active and relevant to the current game.
  • Easy to access, with a preference for web-based tools.
  • Meaningfully different from the other tools already listed.
  • Useful without automating or interfering with gameplay.

If two websites do almost exactly the same thing, we would rather recommend the one we think is better than give you ten identical links.
